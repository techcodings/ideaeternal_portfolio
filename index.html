FAQ

Common Questions

How long does it take to build a project?

+

It depends on the scope. A business website takes 1–2 weeks. A billing application takes 3–5 weeks. A full ERP system can take 6–10 weeks. We provide a specific timeline during our discovery call.

What’s the cost of building a project?

+

Download our pricing guide for detailed ranges. Business websites start from ₹15,000 and full-stack applications from ₹30,000. Contact us for a free estimate tailored to your requirements.

Do you provide post-launch support?

+

Yes. All projects include 30 days of free post-launch support. We also offer affordable monthly maintenance packages for ongoing bug fixes, security updates, and feature additions.

Will I own the code?

+

Yes, 100%. Once delivered and paid, you own all source code, assets, and intellectual property. We provide complete handover documentation.

What technologies do you use?

+

We build with React, Next.js, Node.js, Express, Flask, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, SQLite, Supabase, Three.js, and Vite. We choose the right stack for each project.

How do I get started?

+

Fill out the contact form below or WhatsApp us at +91 9626296000. We’ll schedule a free discovery call to understand your idea, goals, and budget.
