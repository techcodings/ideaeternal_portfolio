FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you’d want to know before starting a project with us.

How long does it take to build a project?

+

It depends on the scope. A business website takes 1–2 weeks. A full-stack web application with backend and database typically takes 4–8 weeks. An AI or mobile product can take 8–16 weeks. We’ll give you a specific timeline during our discovery call after understanding your requirements.

What’s the cost of building a project?

+

Pricing depends on complexity, tech stack, and timeline. Simple websites start from ₹15,000. Full-stack applications with backend, auth, and database start from ₹50,000. AI solutions and complex platforms are scoped individually. Contact us for a free estimate — we’re always transparent about pricing.

Do you work with clients outside India?

+

Yes. We work with clients globally. All communication is done via email, WhatsApp, and video calls. Payments can be made via bank transfer, UPI, or international payment methods. We’ve successfully delivered projects for clients in different time zones.

Do you provide post-launch support and maintenance?

+

Yes. All projects include 30 days of free post-launch support. After that, we offer affordable monthly maintenance packages covering bug fixes, performance monitoring, security updates, and feature additions. We don’t disappear after delivery.

What technologies do you use?

+

We primarily build with React.js, React Native, Node.js, Express, Python, FastAPI, Flask, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Firebase. For AI, we use TensorFlow, OpenCV, BERT, and GPT APIs. For cloud, we use AWS (EC2, S3), Docker, and CI/CD pipelines. We always choose the right tool for the job, not just what we know.

Can you integrate AI into our existing system?

+

Absolutely. We specialize in adding AI layers to existing platforms — whether that’s a GPT chatbot, an NLP recommendation engine, an image recognition module, or an automation pipeline. We work with your existing codebase and APIs where possible.

How do I get started?

+

Simply fill out the contact form below, or WhatsApp us directly at +91 9626296000. We’ll schedule a free 30-minute discovery call to understand your idea, goals, and budget — and share our recommended approach. No commitment required.

Will I own the code and intellectual property?

+

Yes, 100%. Once the project is delivered and payment is complete, you own all the source code, assets, and intellectual property. We provide full handover documentation so you or any future developer can continue working on the product.
